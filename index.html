Behind the dashboard

This reproduces the final cumulative-threshold ordinal logistic classifier: 14 binary logistic models generate probabilities for 15 ordered salary buckets. The final L1-regularized model (C = 1.0), training-fitted category encoders, and multi-select vocabulary are bundled with the page. Predictions use raw responses transformed through that saved preprocessing.

Selected original fields: Country, Currency, RoleFunctions, Age, Employment, EmploymentAddl, PurchasePower, SOYears, AIToken, Role, CloudPlatHaveWorkedWith, KnowledgeQ, Industry, OrgSize, AITry. No engineered variables were selected for the final model.
